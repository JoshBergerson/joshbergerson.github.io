// 01 — Motorsport

UC Motorsport
Formula Student

As Electrical Team Lead for the University of Canterbury Motorsport Club, I oversee the full electrical architecture of a Formula Student race car — from harness design through to high-voltage systems integration.

In 2025, our team took 1st Place at the Formula Student Australasia Competition, competing against university teams across the region. The result was the product of months of iterative design, late-night testing sessions, and a team that refused to settle.

In 2026, I am leading development of the first pouch cell accumulator ever used in a UCM electric vehicle — a 600V system replacing the traditional cylindrical cell format. The car will compete in China and Australia later this year.

Formula Student demands you to be an engineer and a manager simultaneously — you're responsible for technical delivery, team coordination, supplier relationships, and competing under pressure on race day. It's the closest thing to working in the automotive industry before you graduate.
